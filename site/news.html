The Story So Far

History

Disarm Goliath are a heavy metal band out of the West Midlands, built on a simple idea: do it yourself, do it properly, and answer to nobody. Formed by bassist and founder Steve Surch, they’ve been unsigned and self-funded from day one — every record, every tour and every daft decision along the way their own.

2001

‘Only The Devil Can Stop Us’ — the debut

The debut album set the template: proper British metal with weight to it, no gimmicks, and a stubborn refusal to wait for permission. The cover was painted by acclaimed fantasy artist Dominic Harman, who would go on to give the whole catalogue its visual thread.

Early Years

Grafters, not chancers

Rather than wait for opportunities, the band made their own. A cold email to promoters in California put Disarm Goliath on stage at Hollywood’s legendary Whisky a Go Go — which led to two self-funded mini-tours of the United States. Along the way came appearances in Metal Hammer, spots on compilation CDs, and a run supporting former Iron Maiden vocalist Blaze Bayley.

2008

‘Man, Machine & Murder’ EP

Released while the band was still built around its early core — before Steve Bell or Anthony Ellis had joined — the EP bridged the raw ambition of the debut and the bigger productions to come. Proof they never stopped writing, on their own terms and their own dime.

2009

A shifting line-up

Vocalist Steve “The Banshee” Bell joined in 2009, with guitarist Anthony Ellis following in 2010 — ahead of Born to Rule — to add a dual-guitar dynamic. Guitarist and mixer Steve Beville became key both on stage and behind the desk. Through every era, founder Steve Surch and drummer Karl Wade were the constant thread — the two who played on every Disarm Goliath album.

2012

‘Born To Rule’

One of the band’s defining statements — Disarm Goliath at their most ambitious, with cover art once again by Dominic Harman. Its reputation grew far enough to earn a vinyl reissue through Germany’s Pure Steel Records, a rare stamp of approval for a self-funded British band.

2016

‘Wisdom & War’

The third album found the band at a confident, mature peak — fifteen years of knowing exactly who they were. Harman completed his run on the artwork.

2018

Losing Karl Wade

Drummer Karl Wade stepped away from the band in 2018, and passed later that year. There was no dramatic split and no farewell — Disarm Goliath simply stepped back, and the catalogue gradually slipped out of reach as the years passed.

2026

The comeback — ‘Fighting Talk’

Now they’re back. The full catalogue has returned to streaming and been reissued the way it always deserved, and years of silence break with a brand-new single, Fighting Talk. Unsigned, self-funded, and still doing it their own way — just as they have since 2001.
